Smart India Hackathon

A nationwide platform where student innovators work together to develop practical solutions for real-world challenges.

About the Hackathon

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a national initiative that encourages students to think creatively and apply technology to important problems. It is organized by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell with the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and participating partners.

Students form teams, study a selected problem statement, and collaborate on a solution.
The experience brings together teamwork, problem-solving, and innovation.

SIH includes software and hardware innovation tracks, giving teams different ways to turn ideas into useful prototypes.

Challenges and Innovation

Problem statements are contributed by public and private organizations. Teams explore the needs behind each challenge and present a thoughtful, technology-enabled response.

Understand

Research the problem, its users, and the real-world context.

Collaborate

Bring different skills together and build as a team.

Create

Design, develop, and demonstrate a proposed solution.

Achievements and Impact

SIH gives students an opportunity to present ideas, learn from mentors, and gain experience working on challenges beyond the classroom.

Every strong solution starts with a team willing to ask questions, test ideas, and keep improving.

Skills and Career Opportunities

Participation can help students build practical skills valued across many fields. The hackathon is a learning and innovation experience; it does not guarantee a placement or job.

Skills you can practice: communication, project planning, coding, design thinking, teamwork, and presenting your work.
